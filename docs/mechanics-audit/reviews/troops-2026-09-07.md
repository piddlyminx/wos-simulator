## Ambusher distribution and transcription limit

Recorded game scores are `2057, 572, -2668, 1547, 2428, 2199, -1731, 1178, -1580, -2209`: mean **+179.30**, sample SD **2006.64**. Current 20% gives **−807.72±1886.84**, p=**0.143893** with 2,000 simulator samples. The baseline's independent 1,000-sample seed gave −778.39±1930.34, p=0.184041. The sizeable mean difference is visible but does not reject the distribution under the current comparison. This is compatibility with a broad distribution, not exact mean agreement.

The 10% candidate gives −3976.17±741.88; the 30% candidate gives +2267.15±1036.80. Each fails with p=0.00005. Removing Ambusher leaves a deterministic −5725, far outside every recorded observation. Thus the existing full-kit battle is meaningfully discriminating without a new capture.

Trial 2 stores attacker **581** and defender **9**, while its per-line metadata sums to attacker **590** and defender **0**. This audit preserves the original signed **572**. Excluding that inconsistent trial only as sensitivity gives current p=0.187341, 10% p=0.00005, and 30% p=0.000200; the inference above is unchanged. Original reported Ambusher counts are retained in the replay artifact as same-battle diagnostics, not independent samples or precise probability denominators. No correction to the fixture was made.

`testcases/emulator_verified/simple_001_nc.json#0` is Lancer-only T8 versus T9: it gives game/current −186 with zero endpoint variance in 1,000 simulator draws. With no Marksman target, it does **not** add positive Ambusher targeting evidence. Likewise, BandsOfSteel hydration in the Ambusher fixture does not prove protection there: the opposing army has no Lancers.

## FC and tier boundaries

T6 retains **MasterBrawler, Charge and RangedStrike**. Current BandsOfSteel, Ambusher and Volley definitions unlock at **tier 7**, so T8/T9 positive evidence alone does not identify the precise boundary. FC skills use the highest satisfied **FC** requirement independently of tier; **there is no T11 gate in current hydration**. The exact `infantry_t1_fc10` and `lancer_t1_fc10` keys in the Gatot corpus were preserved, not silently promoted to T11. Current class aggregation uses the highest tier and FC within a troop class; mixed-tier inheritance has not been established by this review.
