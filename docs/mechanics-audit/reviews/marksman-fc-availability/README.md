The read-only route is Chief avatar → **Troops** → **All** → **Squads**. Squads opens a formation preview with a Save button; leaving with the back arrow saved nothing and deployed nothing. Both instances finish on Troops Preview/All. Training queues were not claimed, no troops were trained or upgraded, no resources were spent, and no troops were healed during this inspection.

Current config gates Crystal Gunpowder atFC3 and Flame Charge atFC8. Those thresholds come from `simulator/config/troop_skills.json`; this inspection does not independently verify their unlock text. The inventory/formation cards expose tier badges and available counts but did not open a skill-detail panel when tapped. Injured troops and unclaimed completed queues were not individually inspected. Thus the finding concerns the available formations shown directly; it makes no inference from hero generation, city level or report stats and does not claim the missing combat mechanics are validated.
